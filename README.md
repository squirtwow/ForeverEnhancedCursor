# Forever Enhanced Cursor

Cursor effects for **World of Warcraft: Forever**: a smooth trail, a ring round
the cursor, cast progress round the cursor and a see-through copy of the game's
pointer that marks where the cursor comes back while you turn or look around. Every effect starts off; turn on the
ones you like in `/fec`.

## Effects
- **Trail**: soft dots placed evenly along a smooth path, fading behind the
  cursor. Dot spacing, lifetime, the most dots, width, height, opacity and an
  X / Y offset, with Only in combat, and extras: glow, shrink as it fades and
  dots turned along the path
- **Colours**: your class colour, one colour, a rainbow, or a gradient of up to
  ten colours of your own, with how many are in use, how often they repeat
  along the trail and how fast they flow out from the cursor. Any colour is
  picked in the addon's own colour picker or typed as hex digits; each colour
  shows at once, and Cancel puts the old one back
- **Cursor ring**: any size and thickness, opacity, and your class colour, a
  colour of your own or the trail's
- **Cast ring**: fills as you cast and drains as you channel, sitting just
  outside the cursor ring
- **Cursor highlight while looking**: the game hides the cursor while you turn
  or move the camera; a see-through copy of its pointer marks where it will
  come back, fingertip on the spot. On its own switch, any size and opacity,
  untinted or with a gentle tint, for the right button, the left or both
- A live preview of every change at the top of each effect's page, drawn
  round the game's own pointer

## Profiles
- Each character gets its own profile; make, copy, rename, delete and switch
  them from the header's menu or the Profiles page, in combat too
- **Account-wide**: mark one profile for every character, new ones too
- **Share**: export a profile as text, or import one and see what it changes
  before making it a new profile or putting it over yours
- **From EraUI**: with EraUI loaded, copy its cursor settings into a profile
  (offered once)
- **Auto-switch**: show another profile for a while, by rule, the first that
  matches deciding: in combat, a mount you list (Needs testing), mounted,
  battleground or arena, dungeon or raid, open world, out of combat, and each
  talent group. Your own pick stays yours, and nothing switches while the
  window is open. Off until you tick it

## General
- The window's accent colour and the minimap button
- **Reset**: put this profile, or everything, back to the defaults. It asks
  which first; This profile keeps its name, account-wide mark and
  Auto-switch picks, and Everything starts again from one new profile, as a
  first install does
- **More from Squirt**: Forever Enhanced Cooldown Manager (two screenshots,
  full size while the mouse is on one; open it from here if you have it, or
  copy its CurseForge or GitHub link), and Forever Enhanced Raid Frames,
  coming soon

## Commands
- `/fec` (or `/fecursor`) opens the settings
- `/fec reset` asks whether to reset this profile or everything
- `/fec tour` starts the tour of the window
- `/fec new` shows What's new
- `/fec discord` shows the Discord invite

EraUI has cursor effects of its own; while both are on you'll see both, and
the pages say so. Turn EraUI's off in `/era` to see only these.
