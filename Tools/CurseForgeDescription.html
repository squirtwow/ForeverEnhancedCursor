🖱️ Forever Enhanced Cursor

💬 Support, bugs & ideas: join our Discord to get help, report issues and suggest features.

Cursor effects for World of Warcraft: Forever: a smooth trail, a ring round the cursor, cast progress round it and a see-through pointer that marks where the cursor comes back while you look around. Every effect starts off, so you only get the ones you turn on.

✦ Highlights

  • ✨ Cursor trail: smooth, evenly spaced dots that fade behind the cursor, with glow, shrink and dots turned along the path
  • 🌈 Trail colours: your class colour, one colour, a rainbow, or a gradient of up to ten colours that can repeat and flow
  • ⭕ Cursor ring round the cursor, so it's easy to find
  • ⏳ Cast ring: your cast progress round the cursor
  • 🎯 Cursor marker: a bullseye, crosshair, dot, diamond, star or your class icon on your pointer
  • 👆 Cursor highlight while looking: the game hides the cursor while you turn or move the camera, and a see-through copy of its pointer marks where it comes back
  • 👀 Live preview at the top of the Trail, Colours and Rings pages, drawn round the game's own pointer
  • 🎨 A colour picker of its own: each colour shows at once, and Cancel puts the old one back
  • 👥 Profiles: one per character or one for all, shared as text, and switched for you by rules
  • 🎓 A quick tour of the window on a first install, and on the General page any time
  • 🗺️ Minimap button: click for the settings, right-click for What's new

✨ Trail

  • Soft dots placed evenly along a smooth path, fading behind the cursor
  • Dot spacing, how long each dot lasts, the most dots, width, height, opacity and an X / Y offset
  • Only in combat, for the trail
  • Extras: glow, shrink as it fades and dots turned along the path

🌈 Colours

  • Class, one colour, a rainbow, or a gradient of up to ten colours of your own
  • Colours in use, Phases (how often they repeat along the trail) and Colour speed (how fast they flow out from the cursor)
  • Pick any colour in the addon's own colour picker or type it as hex digits

⭕ Rings

  • Cursor ring: size, thickness and opacity, in your class colour, a colour of your own or the trail's
  • Cast ring: fills as you cast and drains as you channel, sitting just outside the cursor ring. Size, opacity and the same colours
  • While looking: a see-through copy of the game's pointer, its fingertip where the cursor will come back. Size, opacity, untinted or a gentle tint, a gentle pulse, and for the right button, the left or both
  • Each tab says whether its effect is on, and the tick at the top of the tab turns it on

🎯 Marker

  • Cursor marker: a bullseye, crosshair, dot, diamond, star or your class icon, centred on your pointer
  • Size and opacity, in your class colour, a colour of your own or the trail's, with Only in combat

👥 Profiles

  • Each character gets its own profile. New, copy, rename, delete and switch from the header's menu or the Profiles page, in combat too
  • Account-wide: one profile for every character, new ones too
  • Share a profile as text, and import one as a new profile or over yours, seeing what it changes first
  • From EraUI: with EraUI loaded, copy its cursor settings into a profile, offered once

🔁 Auto-switch

  • Show another profile for a while, by rule: in combat, a mount you list (Needs testing), mounted, battleground or arena, dungeon or raid, open world, not in combat, and each talent group
  • The first rule that matches decides. Your own pick stays yours, and nothing switches while the window is open
  • Off until you tick it

⚙️ General

  • The window's accent colour and the minimap button
  • Reset: this profile or everything back to the defaults, asked first
  • Help: take the tour, What's new, and the Discord button for bugs and ideas
  • More from Squirt: Forever Enhanced Cooldown Manager, with screenshots, and Forever Enhanced Raid Frames, coming soon
  • Hover anything in the window and the footer says what it does

💬 Commands

  • /fec (or /fecursor) opens the settings, also under Options > AddOns
  • /fec reset asks whether to reset this profile or everything
  • /fec tour starts the tour of the window
  • /fec new shows What's new
  • /fec discord shows the Discord invite

EraUI has cursor effects of its own. While both are on you'll see both, and the pages say so: turn EraUI's off in /era to see only these.
